About the startup

Search once. Compare everywhere. Buy through one conversation.

ShopBot is an AI-powered shopping comparison and checkout assistant. It is not a marketplace.

Problem
Consumers waste time checking multiple platforms to find the best deal: search Amazon, search Flipkart, search Croma, compare, decide, then check out.
Solution
A conversational assistant that aggregates listings from many platforms and lets people compare, choose a seller and check out in one chat.
Target customers
Digital-first Indian consumers aged roughly 18 to 35.
Initial categories
Electronics, fashion, home appliances, with groceries and beauty through quick-commerce and beauty platforms.
Platforms in this demo
Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, BigBasket, Nykaa, Myntra, Croma, Reliance Digital.
Alternatives today
Amazon, Flipkart, Google Shopping, price comparison sites, individual brand websites.
Differentiation
A conversation-first experience in a familiar messaging interface, with no tab-hopping.

Today

Search Amazon → Search Flipkart → Search Croma → Compare prices → Decide → Checkout

With ShopBot

Tell ShopBot what you want → ShopBot compares options → You choose → Simulated checkout

How we make money

User→ShopBot→Merchant

The primary model is a commission or referral fee: when a user buys through a partner platform, that platform pays ShopBot a share of the sale. The user pays the same listed price.

Other revenue streams (future)

  • Affiliate commission on completed purchases
  • Sponsored product placement, always labelled
  • Merchant referral fees for new customers
  • Premium subscription for price alerts and deal tracking
  • Merchant analytics on price position and demand

This prototype does not earn money and does not connect to any real platform, payment system or WhatsApp account. It is a simulation of the experience and the concept.
